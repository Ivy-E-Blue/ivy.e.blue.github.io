About the Author

Medha Nair, writing under the pen name Ivy. E Blue, is a storyteller who loves creating big mysteries, high-stakes adventures, and worlds filled with elemental magic. Ivy has spent countless hours bringing the characters of Camp Orion to life, falling in love with the process of building a story from scratch.


When not daydreaming about new plot twists or hanging out in fictional worlds, Ivy can be found taking a break outside, exploring new ideas, going to school, and just enjoying life. This is their first novel.
